The dosing-skid case

120 bolts. Two suppliers. One revision.

01 · Confirm the customer requirement

The buyer reviews the saved email and PDF and confirms 120 M8 × 35 A2-70 hex bolts.

02 · Prepare the supplier RFQ

The buyer approves recipients and releases a draft email with a prefilled Excel reply form. The RFQ reference links subsequent replies.

03 · Compare cost and historical evidence

Supplier A costs OMR 42.000. Supplier B has a cheaper unit price but initially costs OMR 50.000 because of its minimum. Three comparable past purchases put A’s unit price 40% above the OMR 0.250 median.

04 · Recheck the quote after the supplier revision

B reduces its minimum to 120 pieces, dropping total cost to OMR 30.000. The buyer selects B. The previous approved quote is blocked from release and the buyer prepares a new version.

05 · Approve and record issuance

The new quote is OMR 37.560 excluding VAT. The downloaded email contains the exact reviewed PDF. Staff record manual issuance and a confirmed purchase separately.
