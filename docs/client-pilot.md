# Client pilot scope

## First outcome

Take one product category from a customer enquiry to an approved customer quotation. Demonstrate that the buyer can trace each quoted line back to the requirement, the selected supplier terms, and the pricing decision.

- Start with representative, permitted enquiry PDFs and returned supplier spreadsheets. Agree the fields that need staff confirmation.
- Configure one supplier directory, currency and pricing policy, including MOQ, margin and quote validity rules.
- Test a successful quote and exceptions for missing terms, revised prices, and expired offers.
- Agree who approves supplier and customer drafts. Review any requirement for authentication, audit retention, and mail integration before deployment.

The public project uses fictional Oman trading data. It demonstrates saved-file replay and manual sending. A client pilot would need its own acceptance cases and decisions about data access and deployment.
